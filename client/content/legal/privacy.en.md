# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

This Privacy Policy (Aviso de Privacidad) explains how **Disrupting Labs** ("we", "us"), operator of **QuickSanta** (quicksanta.com), collects and uses personal data, in accordance with Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) and its regulations.

**Data controller:** Disrupting Labs · Contact: [alex@disruptinglabs.com](mailto:alex@disruptinglabs.com)

## 1. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email address, preferred language | You, when you sign in |
| Exchange data | Exchange name, date, budget, location, participant names and emails, exclusion rules, wishlists, draw results | Organizers and participants |
| Billing | Workspace name, subscription status, card brand, last 4 digits and expiry, invoices | You and our payment processor (Stripe) |
| Technical | IP address, browser user agent, sign-in and activity logs | Automatically, when you use the service |

We **never** receive or store your full card number or CVC; card details go directly to Stripe. We do not collect sensitive personal data as defined by the LFPDPPP.

## 2. Why we use it

**Primary purposes** (needed to provide the service):

- Create and secure your account with one-time sign-in codes.
- Run gift exchanges: send invitations, perform fair draws, show each person only their own match and wishlist.
- Process Pro subscriptions, invoices and payment-method updates.
- Prevent fraud and abuse, keep audit logs, and comply with legal obligations.

**Secondary purposes** (you can object at any time by emailing us): product analytics and occasional service announcements. Objecting does not affect your use of QuickSanta.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with processors that help us run QuickSanta, under confidentiality obligations:

- **Vercel** (hosting), **PingCAP / TiDB Cloud** (database), **Resend** (transactional email), **Stripe** (payments).

Some processors are located outside Mexico (e.g. the United States); these transfers are necessary to provide the service. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data when required by law or a competent authority.

## 4. Privacy of the draw

Assignments are private: only the giver can see who they are gifting. Organizers see aggregate status (for example, how many people have revealed their match), not who drew whom. Platform administrators access data only to operate, support and secure the service.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

We use one essential, HttpOnly session cookie (`qs_sid`) to keep you signed in, and local storage to remember your language and unfinished wizard drafts. Stripe may set cookies on its secure card fields for fraud prevention. We do not use advertising cookies.

## 6. Retention

We keep account and exchange data while your account or workspace is active. Sign-in codes are deleted after 2 days; expired sessions are deleted 7 days after they expire and signed-out sessions after 30 days. Unfinished exchange drafts (saved in your browser and, when you are signed in, in your account) are deleted 30 days after their last edit, or as soon as you launch or delete them. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

## 7. Your ARCO rights

You may **Access**, **Rectify**, **Cancel** (delete) or **Object** to the processing of your personal data, and revoke your consent, by emailing [alex@disruptinglabs.com](mailto:alex@disruptinglabs.com) with your name, the email you use on QuickSanta, and a description of your request. We will reply within 20 business days. You may also contact Mexico's data protection authority if you believe your rights were infringed.

## 8. Children

QuickSanta is intended for people 18 or older, or minors using it under the supervision of a parent or guardian who organizes the exchange.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), hashed sign-in codes and session tokens, role-based access, tenant isolation and audit logs. No system is 100% secure; we will notify you of any breach that significantly affects your rights.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new date and, for significant changes, notify you by email or in the app.
